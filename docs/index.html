What it is like to use.

You bring the add-ons. Nebula makes the pages, finds the pictures and the subtitles, and takes care of the small things that make an evening of episodes easy.

Subtitles, sorted by language

Open the panel and every subtitle for the film is there, grouped by language with a count, whether it came with the file or from an add-on. Timing and size sit next to the list, so a track that runs early can be nudged without leaving the picture.

The next episode is already lined up

Nebula remembers which source you picked for a show. Near the end it fetches the next episode from the same place, so Play now starts it straight away, and if you do nothing it rolls on by itself.

Playback that keeps out of the way

Press play and it plays. Nebula skips the recap and the opening when it knows where they are, and the closing credits bring the next episode forward. A whole season in one sitting, without reaching for the scrub bar.

  • Skips intros and recaps, on its own if you prefer
  • Finds the next episode's stream while you are still watching
  • A sleep timer for the last episode of the night
  • Picture quality set once: automatic, best available, or data saver

Pause, and the details show up

Leave it paused for a moment and the title, the synopsis and the time left fade in, with the time it will finish. The clock sits in the corner while the controls are up, so you can tell whether there is room for one more.

A profile without an email

Pick a handle and a password. Your add-ons, progress, list and ratings follow you to every screen you sign in on. A TV signs in with a short code that you approve from your phone, so there is nothing to type with the remote.

Works with the add-ons you already have.

Catalogs and streams come from the add-ons you install. Paste a link once, or scan a code from your phone to add one to the TV. Nebula asks each add-on for four things and lays them out the way a streaming app would.

Catalogs

Rows on Home, in the order you choose. Hide the ones you never open.

Title pages

Artwork, cast, ratings, seasons and episodes, with your place marked.

Streams

Every add-on's streams in one list, labelled by resolution, size and language.

Subtitles

Downloaded, cleaned up and re-timed on the device, in the style you set.

Made in the open.

What Nebula is not.

Nebula is a player. It browses catalogs and plays streams supplied by the add-ons you install, or links you give it, and it is meant for content you are entitled to watch. It does not host, store or distribute media.

Nebula is not affiliated with any add-on, catalog, source or content provider.
