What it is like to use

The polish of a big streaming app, pointed at your own sources.

Nebula does not hand you a catalog. You bring the add-ons. What it adds is everything around them.

Skip intro

It knows where the titles are.

A pill appears over an episode's recap and opening titles. Press it, or let Nebula skip them for you. The closing credits bring the next episode forward, so a six-episode evening never touches the scrub bar.

Subtitles

Every subtitle, one panel.

Languages with a count of what is available, each language's tracks and where they came from, and the timing and look of the words beside them. Nudge a track that arrives early, make it larger for the sofa, and it stays that way on every screen.

Instant next episode

The next one is ready before this one ends.

Nebula remembers which source you chose for a show and finds the next episode's stream while you are still watching. Play now starts it at once, from the same source and quality, with no list to pick from.

The quiet parts

Pause, and it tells you where you are.

A moment after you pause, the title, synopsis, time left and what plays next fade in. The clock top right says when it ends. On a TV, one toolbar holds everything the remote needs: next, subtitles, audio, quality, speed, a sleep timer and the party.

Evenings, sorted

A sleep timer that pauses after 15 to 90 minutes, or when the episode you are on ends, and rides along into the next one.

Profiles without an email

A handle and a password. Add-ons, progress, your list and ratings follow you to every screen. A TV signs in with a short code you approve from your phone.

Watch together

A five-letter code or a QR puts friends on the same second, live streams included, with reactions and who is in the room.

Decrypts on the device

Protected streams play through the platform's own pipeline, on the TV, the PC or the phone. Nothing is re-streamed through a server.

Made for the couch

Full remote navigation, an icon rail, a full-bleed featured board, and a light paint mode for TV chipsets. Your accent colour, your row order.

Keyboard first on the desktop

Press ? for every shortcut: K, J, L for playback, S, A, Q for the pickers, N for the next episode, Z for the sleep timer, digits to jump.

Private by design

Nothing to sign up for.

Nebula works with no account at all. A profile is optional, and it asks for a handle and a password, never an email, a phone number or a name.

  • Your add-on list, progress and settings live on your device unless you create a profile.
  • No trackers, no analytics, no advertising identifiers on any screen.
  • Streams decrypt on the device. The server only relays sync, parties and the odd add-on that blocks browsers.
  • Everything the server ever sees is written down in the privacy page, from the code.
Made in the open

Every screen's app is public on GitHub.

Read it, build it yourself, or send a patch. Releases are built by the repositories' own workflows, so what you download is what you can read.
