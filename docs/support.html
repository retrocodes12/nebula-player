Support Nebula

Nebula is free, with no ads and no account required, and every feature stays free for everyone. If it earns a place in your evenings, you can chip in once — and get a small thank-you back.

Supporting keeps the sync server, the site and the sports feed running. Nothing here is a subscription: you pay once and it is yours for good.

Buying for — the thank-you lands on that profile the moment the payment clears.
Not signed in on this browser? That is fine: after paying you get a one-time code to type into Settings › Support on any signed-in device. Opening this page from the app (Settings › Support) skips the code.

      Questions

      Is anything locked without paying? No. Every feature is free for everyone and stays that way. Supporters get thank-yous — a mark, colours, a name on a list — not features.

      Does it expire? No. One payment, yours for good. Paying a higher tier later simply raises you; you never pay twice for the same thing.

      Where does it show? On your Nebula profile, so on every device you sign in to — TV, phone, computer. The wall is opt-in: your name appears only if you switch it on in Settings › Support.

      Something wrong with a purchase? Open an issue on the Nebula repository with the order number from your receipt — never your card details.
